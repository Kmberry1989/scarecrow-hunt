First Friday Scarecrow Scavenger Hunt

New to QR codes? Let’s practice!

The square below is a real QR code — give it a try right now.

Try it — it takes about ten seconds:

  1. Open your phone’s camera app. No special app needed — the regular camera does it.
  2. Point it at the square above and hold steady for a moment.
  3. Tap the notification or link that pops up on your screen.
iPhoneThe Camera app scans automatically. If nothing pops up, check Settings > Camera > Scan QR Codes is on.
AndroidMost Android cameras scan automatically too. If nothing pops up, look for a QR mode or “Scan QR codes” in your Camera settings.

Did it work? Hooray — you’re ready to hunt! Each scarecrow’s pumpkin has a QR code just like this one. Scan it to collect its letter.
No phone? No problem — the back of each pumpkin shows its letter and blank number, so you can play on paper and still enter the prize drawing.

Questions? Just ask anyone here — we’re happy to help!
