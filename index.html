How the Scarecrow Scavenger Hunt Works

Friday, October 2 — downtown Kokomo

5:30–8:00 PM is the official First Friday event time — but the hunt runs on your schedule. The QR codes stay up after the event, so you’re welcome to play at your own convenience, for as long as the QR codes remain posted.

  • Grab a hunt card — free at Artworks Gallery (210 N Main St) or Fired Arts Studio (111 W Sycamore St).
  • Find the 21 scarecrows — start at any scarecrow and visit in any order. Scan each pumpkin’s QR code with your phone’s camera to bank its letter automatically and unlock the full experience.

    New to QR codes? They look like this — point your phone’s camera at one and tap the link that pops up.
    No phone? No problem — the back of each pumpkin shows its letter and blank number, so you can play on paper and still enter the prize drawing. Details in the FAQ.
  • Turn in your finished card — drop it off at the front desk of Artworks Gallery. Every completed card enters the Mystery Prize Basket drawing — late finishes count, too (Artworks Gallery takes cards until 9 PM).

A family-friendly evening — kids are encouraged to come hunt with a parent or guardian. Please use crosswalks, and bring a phone and/or flashlight after dark.
