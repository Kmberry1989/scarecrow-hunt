🍂 Thank You to Our First Friday Sponsors!

🍂 How the hunt works

First Friday, October 2 — downtown Kokomo

  1. Grab a passport — free hunt card at Artworks Gallery (210 N Main St) or Fired Arts Studio (111 W Sycamore St).
  2. Find all 21 scarecrows — scan each pumpkin’s QR code with your phone to collect its letter.
  3. Crack the phrase — unscramble the secret phrase, turn in your card at either spot, and you’re entered in the Mystery Prize Basket drawing.

Please use crosswalks, and bring a phone and/or flashlight after dark. 🔦
