# First Friday Scarecrow Scavenger Hunt — digital kit

Everything is built. Three setup steps, about 20 minutes total.

## What's in here

- `site/` — the 21 landing pages. Upload these to GitHub Pages (step 1).
- `site/logos/` — drop sponsor logos here as `logo-01.png` … `logo-21.png`
  (number = scarecrow number). They appear automatically on that scarecrow's page.
- `print/qr-print.pdf` — 8.5x11 sheets with the 21 QR cards. Print, laminate,
  cut along the dashed lines, attach to the plaques. **The cards do NOT show the
  letter** — hunters only get it by scanning.
- `print/qr-01.png` … `qr-21.png` — individual QR images if you need them.
- `answer-key.md` — PRIVATE. Which letter lives at which scarecrow.

## Step 1 — put the pages online (GitHub Pages, free)

1. Create a new GitHub repo named `scarecrow-hunt` (public).
2. Upload the *contents* of the `site/` folder to the repo root
   (so the repo contains `s01.html` … `s21.html`, `index.html`, `logos/`).
3. Repo Settings → Pages → Source: **Deploy from a branch**,
   branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`. Save.
4. Wait ~2 minutes, then open `https://kmberry1989.github.io/scarecrow-hunt/s01.html`
   on your phone. If it loads, you're live.
5. Scan 2–3 printed QR codes with your phone camera to confirm they open
   the right pages.

If you use a different GitHub username or repo name, tell me and I'll regenerate
all 21 QR codes in seconds — don't print until the URLs are final.

## Step 2 — scan counting (GoatCounter, free) — ALREADY WIRED IN

The tracking snippet for your GoatCounter site (`kmberry1989.goatcounter.com`)
is baked into all 21 pages. Nothing to paste.

1. After deploying (step 1), scan 2–3 QR codes with your phone. Within a minute,
   those hits should appear in your GoatCounter dashboard — proving counting works.
   (You can delete test data later in the dashboard.)
2. If you test-scan every code once before the event, even better: you'll confirm
   all 21 pages count correctly.

After First Friday: open `https://kmberry1989.goatcounter.com` → your site →
the **Pages** report lists hits per page (`/s01.html` … `/s21.html`).
Match page numbers to scarecrows with `answer-key.md` — those are your raw
scan counts per location for the assignment.

## Step 3 — print and mount

Print `print/qr-print.pdf`, laminate, cut apart, attach one card per plaque.
That's it — hunters just point their phone camera at the code. No app,
no login, no typing.
